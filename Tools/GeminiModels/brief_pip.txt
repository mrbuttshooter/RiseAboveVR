SUBJECT: "Pip", a tiny friendly companion bird-drone for a VR game - a round, chubby, glowing sky-blue songbird about 0.18 m across, like a cute mascot. Big shiny black eyes with two white highlights each, small orange beak, cream belly, tiny feet tucked under, a little curl of feathers on top of the head, rosy cheek dots. The body material should be bright sky blue (0.35, 0.8, 1.0) with a soft emission of the same colour (strength 0.6). Build the WINGS as SEPARATE objects named "WingL" and "WingR" (small rounded wings held slightly out to the sides, each wing's origin at its shoulder so it can rotate to flap). Everything else is ONE object named "Pip". Parent the wings to Pip. Origin of Pip at the centre of its body (not the feet). 3000-8000 triangles total.
